Research · MATLAB / Simscape

BEV Battery Thermal Management System

A 9-phase closed-loop thermal simulation of the Chalmers/Volvo BEV Loop 3 cooling circuit, built from the ground up in MATLAB Simscape. The model replicates the full coolant circuit — pump, cold plate, radiator, and hysteresis relay control — with linear temperature-dependent battery internal resistance and a WLTC drive cycle heat load profile.

Validated vs. published data WLTC drive cycle 50/50 EG/water coolant

Hysteresis relay control with thresholds at 310.65 K / 303.15 K. Fixed thermal resistance radiator (R = 0.001515 K/W). Nusselt number Nu = 7.54 for parallel-plate laminar flow. Presented to and accepted by research advisor Prof. Hongwei Sun.
