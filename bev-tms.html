Architecture

BEV Loop 3 — System Overview

  • 01
    Thermal Liquid (TL) Domain Simscape's TL library couples pressure-flow hydraulics with enthalpy-based energy transport. Mass and energy balances enforced simultaneously across all components.
  • 02
    Battery Thermal Mass — Q = I²R Lumped capacitance model: m = 300 kg, cp = 900 J/(kg·K). Arrhenius temperature-dependent internal resistance — more physically accurate than a fixed-R assumption.
  • 03
    Cold Plate — Pipe (TL), Nu = 7.54 Fixed Nusselt number corresponding to fully-developed laminar flow between parallel plates under uniform heat flux. h computed internally from Nu, coolant conductivity, and hydraulic diameter.
  • 04
    Hysteresis Relay Controller Relay block: pump ON at 310.65 K, OFF at 303.15 K. Minimum 1 rad/s maintained at OFF threshold for solver stability. Avoids parasitic power draw when cooling not needed.
  • 05
    Radiator + WLTC Drive Cycle Fixed R_rad = 0.001515 K/W, T_amb = 293.15 K. WLTC velocity profile → C-rate → I²R heat load. Urban (150 A), highway (80 A), regenerative braking (−30 A).

Simulation Results

Heat generation — WLTC pattern match
High-load current spikes early, sustained steady load mid-cycle, near-zero gaps during regenerative braking — consistent shape with Dinakar & Rajeeve (2016) reference.
Battery temperature — correct relay behaviour
Starts at 333.15 K, pump activates immediately, temperature drops to 303.15 K OFF threshold. Sawtooth cycling begins as drive cycle heat rebuilds — matches Fig 4-4 qualitatively.
Coolant flow — validates relay logic
Active flow (~0.3 m/s) when pump ON, near-zero during OFF periods. Pump cycles more frequently during high-speed WLTC phases as expected.
Coolant temperature — heat rejection validated
Coolant lags battery temperature as heat conducts through cold plate first. Stabilises near 303–305 K — confirms radiator pathway functioning correctly.

What We're Trying to Achieve

Research Objectives

  • Close the fixed-Nu gap — the replication model used Nu = 7.54 throughout. The developing-flow Nusselt number is significantly higher near the pipe inlet every time the pump activates. The Hausen correlation captures this transient effect dynamically.
  • Smoother temperature control — hysteresis relay produces characteristic sawtooth oscillations. A PID controller eliminates on/off switching by modulating pump speed continuously, reducing temperature overshoot and energy consumption.
  • Multi-cell resolution — moving from a single lumped thermal mass to a 12-cell pack enables cell-level temperature gradients to be observed and managed, which is critical for real pack design.
  • Path to electronics cooling — the same Simscape loop architecture is directly transferable to rack-level data centre CDU systems by replacing the battery pack with chip-level heat sources, enabling cross-domain research.

Known Limitations

  • Fixed Nu = 7.54 does not capture thermally developing flow near pipe inlet each time pump activates — Nu is considerably higher in the entry region. Addressed in Phase 11.
  • Idealised pump at constant shaft speed — real centrifugal pumps follow a flow-head performance curve with transient ramp-up/down. Addressed in Phase 12.
  • Fixed R_rad regardless of vehicle speed — in reality, airside heat rejection increases with vehicle speed and fan operation.
  • Loop 3 in isolation — thermal interactions with cabin HVAC and powertrain loops are not modelled.
  • Approximated WLTC current profile — derived from velocity plot via simplified tractive energy model; not directly measured.
Independent Research

Open Questions

  • PID tuning — Kp, Ki, Kd values need systematic calibration to avoid overshoot or oscillation in the pump speed response.
  • Cell-to-cell coupling — thermal interaction between adjacent cells in the 12-cell pack needs validation against higher-fidelity CFD.
  • Coolant property variation — pure water assumed for Phase 10 onward; temperature-dependent property updates needed for accuracy at elevated temperatures.
  • Extension to CDU — rack-level heat source characterisation (chip TDP profiles) needs to be defined before the architecture transfer is meaningful.
